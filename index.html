Who We Are

Faith, Voice & The Word

Ajey Cjey is an online Christian and Gospel-based YouTube channel rooted in Uganda and reaching the world. We are a platform for young people to express themselves through music, spoken word, poetry, and the power of Scripture — while extending the Kingdom of God through the most powerful tool of this generation: the internet.


We believe every young person deserves a space where their God-given gifts are celebrated, their faith is strengthened, and their purpose is revealed. Ajey Cjey is that space.

Mission & Vision

Reaching Every Soul

Our mission is to minimize the impact of secular social media by establishing platforms where young people can encounter God and be equipped to live for Christ.

"Impacting youth life through social media by extending the Gospel of Christ to the world."
01
Reach the Unreachable

Utilizing the internet to reach young people who may not have access to local churches — bringing the Gospel directly to their screens.

02
Equip New Believers

Providing tools, resources, and teaching to help new believers grow in their faith and live an authentic Christian life.

03
Platform for Young Voices

Hosting young creators on our YouTube channel — giving them a stage to express themselves and develop their God-given gifts.

04
Showcase the Church

Helping people discover our church community — its location, programs, vision, and the living body of believers behind this platform.
